HOW TO PLAY

🟢 Got it — guessed correctly. Goes green and advances.

🟡 Pass — skip for now. Goes yellow and returns later.

🔴 Missed — mark as missed. Goes red, but you can retry it.

You have to turn all ten green before time runs out. After the first pass through A–J (or another consecutive set), missed words return automatically.
