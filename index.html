⚡ Word Dash · How to play STEP 1 OF 4

Describe it. Guess it.

A fast word-guessing race for two or more people.

👥 Two players, two jobs

Describer: Explain the word on screen without saying the word itself.
Guesser: Listen and shout out the answer.

🔤 One letter at a time

Each answer starts with its highlighted letter. For example, for C and the word Chocolate, you might say: “A sweet brown treat you eat.”

👍 Got itThey guessed correctly. Green! Move to the next letter.
⏭ PassSkip it for now. Yellow words come back later.
👎 MissedWrong answer? Mark it red and try again if there’s time.

⏱️ Beat the countdown

Press Start to reveal the words and begin the timer. It beeps every second and gets more urgent in the last 10 seconds. Get every word green before zero to win!

🔊 “Sounds like…”Try a rhyme, or a word with a similar sound.
🧩 “Break it into parts…”“The first part sounds like…” can help with a tricky answer.
🎯 Start with a category“It’s a type of animal…” or “You’d find this in a kitchen…”
🏃 Keep movingIf you’re stuck, press Pass and circle back if there’s time.

📱 One device

Play together using one screen. The describer sees the words and taps the answer buttons.

👥 Duo mode

Play on two devices. One creates a room; the other joins with a code. The guesser only sees the letter.

🌐 Duo works over the internet, so both devices must have an active network connection.
