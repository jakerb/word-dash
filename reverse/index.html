Reverse
🔁 Reverse · How to play STEP 1 OF 4

🎙 Say a celebrity

On your turn you're secretly shown a famous name. Record yourself saying it normally.

🔁 Copy the backwards sound

The name disappears and the game plays your recording in reverse. Copy that strange sound as closely as you can, and record your copy.

1You see John Cena and say it.
2Reversed, it sounds like “Anek Nohj”.
3You say “Anek Nohj” back into the game.
4The game reverses your copy for the next player. It should sound a bit like John Cena!
5They guess who it is out loud.
✓ Got it!The guesser named the celebrity. The player who recorded scores +1.
✗ Didn't get itNo points this time.

🔄 Pass it round

The guesser takes the next turn, passing it on to the person beside them. Turns go round the circle, and the first player to 10 points wins.

🎤 Allow the microphoneYour browser will ask the first time you record. Recordings are only kept for the current turn and never leave your device.
🤫 Keep it secretDon't let the next player see the name or hear your first recording.
🗣 Overact itExaggerate the backwards vowels and keep the rhythm. Listen as many times as you like before recording your copy.
🔇 Find a quiet spotHold the phone close and say the name clearly. Background noise makes the reversed sound harder to copy.
